FREQUENTLY ASKED

Got Questions? We Have Answers

How long does it take to build a website? +

A typical business website takes 2-4 weeks depending on complexity. Larger projects with custom features (e-commerce, dashboards, APIs) can take 6-12 weeks. We always provide a detailed timeline before starting.

How much does a project cost? +

Pricing depends on scope, features, and complexity. We offer competitive rates for startups and businesses. Contact us for a free consultation and detailed quote tailored to your needs.

Do you provide support after delivery? +

Yes! All projects include 30 days of free bug-fix support after delivery. We also offer extended maintenance packages for ongoing updates and improvements.

What technologies do you work with? +

We specialize in HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React for web; Flutter & Dart for mobile; Java, Spring Boot, PHP for backend; and MySQL & PostgreSQL for databases. We always choose the best stack for your project.

Do you work with clients outside Egypt? +

Absolutely! We work remotely with clients worldwide. We communicate via WhatsApp, email, or video calls, and adjust to your timezone for meetings.

Can you take over an existing project? +

Yes, we can review existing code, fix issues, add new features, or completely refactor it. Send us your project details and we'll provide a full assessment.
